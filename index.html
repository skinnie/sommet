The reason

Some tools are worth keeping alive.

There are newer watches — more accurate, more comfortable, more everything. I enjoy them too. 

But an Ambit is like driving a classic car: it's home.

Newer watches have the convenience of changing settings on the go through your smartphone. Ambits lost that. Because of it, a lot of watches went into a drawer. Some went into landfill.

Sommet brings those abilities back, and adds a lot on top. The whole point is to keep these classic instruments — and the old computers and phones that run the app — out of the bin and out on the trail a little longer.

Sommet also brings the desktop back to these watches — whether you love a big screen or you're now into the “dumb phone” scene, Sommet has you covered.

At heart they're standalone GPS watches — they work on their own on the trail, no phone required — for anyone who wants something simpler, offline and their own.

The field kit

Everything the app can do.

Grouped the way you actually use it — heading out, coming back, keeping the watch alive. 

01

Out on the trail

navigation · settings

Routes

Send a GPX route to your wrist with a preview map, its distance and climb. Remove routes, and export every route on the watch to GPX at once.

Points of interest

Add waypoints by searching a map or typing coordinates, remove them, and export the POIs on the watch. A Traverse gets its own 48 POI types, hunting and fishing ones included.

Orbital GPS data

Push the latest satellite almanac so the watch finds GPS — and GLONASS — far faster outdoors.

Sport modes

A full editor: create, delete,rename modes, set auto-lap and HR limits, choose sensors, lay out the data screens, choose apps, etc 

Watch settings

Read and write units and personal info.

Device manual

Download the official PDF manual for your exact watch, straight from the app.

Route planner

Plan a route on the map and see its climb and the weather along it. Download map tiles to use the map offline.

Race plan & roadbook extra

Plan a brevet or a long ride from a GPX or a roadbook: controls, stops, weather and climbs, with a roadbook PDF.

Copy to watch

Copy sport modes and settings from one watch, or from a backup, to another.

02

Back at basecamp

activities · training

Activities

Download recorded workouts and view them on a map with an elevation profile, a chart that follows the map, time in zones and the weather on the day.

Export & upload

Save as GPX or FIT, one or all at once, or send to Strava, Runalyze, intervals.icu and Garmin Connect.

Calendar & totals

A visual training diary of the days you moved, plus lifetime totals with a few fun facts.

Training coach extra

A readiness “traffic light” and a chat coach that reads your own data — offline for the basics.

HRV & wellness extra

Heart-rate variability from the watch's raw beats; resting HR & weight can also come from intervals.icu, from your other devices.

Gear tracker extra

Track bikes, shoes and parts with maintenance reminders before something wears out on you.

Training Program

A calendar of dated workouts, made in the app or imported from intervals.icu, put on the watch at each sync. Pick the day's workout from the watch's own WORKOUT menu.

Health & sleep extra

Steps, activity calories and recovery time week by week, and a sleep score.

Weight extra

Weigh-ins from an Etekcity Bluetooth scale with a body-fat estimate, sent to intervals.icu if you want.

03

Keep it alive

backup · repair

Backup & restore

Safe backups of routes, POIs, sport modes and firmware to a folder, or to Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive.

Firmware flasher

Install firmware over USB.

Heart-rate sensor

Connect a Suunto HR chest strap and check its battery level and firmware version.

Interval builder

A visual workout builder for structured intervals — a workout on the Ambit 3, or an app for Ambit 1/2.

Guided interval workouts

A lost Movescount feature, revived for the Ambit 3 — with an intervals.icu-to-Ambit converter.

Kailash travel log extra

Read travel history, countries & places visited, set your Home Location.

Sommet Sync extra

One shared database between your computers and your phone, through your own server (a NAS).

Demo mode

Explore the whole app with a sample watch and sample data, no watch needed.

Update notice

The app tells you when a newer Sommet is out.

No fine print

A few honest limitations.

  • BluetoothWorks fully on Android and iOS. On the desktop it is an opt-in experiment: tested on Linux with mixed results depending on the computer's Bluetooth hardware, not tested on macOS or Windows.
  • FirmwareSommet can flash firmware — tested on five devices across different operating systems — but SuuntoLink is still the recommended tool for routine updates; the flasher is an emergency fallback.
  • Training plansBack on the Ambit 3: the day's workout is in the sport's WORKOUT menu. A normal start of that sport stays a free run, and the optional auto-start on a training day is silent on stock firmware.
  • TestingTested and verified on real hardware: Ambit 1, 2, 3r/3s/3 Peak, Traverse and Kailash. The rest of the range shares the same internals and is expected to work, but hasn't all been confirmed. The Traverse Alpha's own features (shot detection, its POI types) were checked in an emulator.
  • As-isProvided with no warranty, for use on your own hardware at your own risk. If a watch ever misbehaves, SuuntoLink can always reset it.
Where we stand

Grateful 

Suunto has spent decades building outdoor instruments worth keeping, and their modern watches and app are genuinely excellent — SuuntoLink and the Suunto app serve them well. Sommet exists to complement it, add features or ease of use.

Sommet is an independent, unofficial community project — not affiliated with, nor endorsed by, Suunto or Garmin. It builds on the openambit and opensportsync work, and hopes to give a little back to that community.
