## WHY
- Eyebrow: The reason
- Heading: **Some tools are worth keeping alive.**
- Lead paragraph: There are newer watches — more accurate, more comfortable, more everything. I enjoy them too. But an Ambit is like driving a classic car: it's home. 
- Paragraph 2: Newer watches have the easyness of being able to change settings on the go through our smartphone. Ambits lost that. Due to that a lot of watches went into a drawer. Some went into the landfill.
- Paragraph 3: Sommet brings those abilities back, and adds a lot on top. The whole point is to keep these classic instruments — and the old computers and phones that run the app — out of the bin and out on the trail a little longer.
- Paragraph 4: Sommet also brings the desktop reality to these watches again, either because you love a big screen, or you are now into the "dumb phone" scene, sommet gets you covered.
- Signature line: — built in stubbornness, coffee & good will · vibe-coded with AI + community work
- Pull-quote: "Fortunately we live in wonderful times. With AI and community work — why not get those lost features back?"
- Pull-quote credit: — the whole idea, in one line
- Side paragraph: No login. No account. No cloud you don't control. Your routes, activities and backups live on your own machine, and the app runs almost entirely offline — only the GPS almanac and the online workout builder need the internet.

## EVERYTHING THE APP CAN DO
- Eyebrow: The field kit
- Heading: **Everything the app can do.**
- Intro: Grouped the way you actually use it — heading out, coming back, keeping the watch alive. Marked ◆ are extras that go beyond what the original tools ever offered.

### 01 — Out on the trail  _(sub-label: navigation · settings)_
- **Routes** — Send a GPX route to your wrist with a preview map, and read routes back off the watch to GPX.
- **Points of interest** — Add waypoints by searching a map or typing coordinates. Export the POIs already on the watch.
- **Orbital GPS data** — Push the latest satellite almanac so the watch finds GPS — and GLONASS* — far faster outdoors.
- **Sport modes** — A full editor: rename modes, set auto-lap and HR limits, choose sensors, lay out the data screens.
- **Watch settings** — Read units and personal info.
- **Device manual** — Download the official PDF manual for your exact watch, straight from the app.

### 02 — Back at basecamp  _(sub-label: activities · training)_
- **Activities** — Download recorded workouts, view them on a map with an elevation profile and route replay.
- **Export & upload** — Save as GPX or FIT, or send to Strava, Runalyze and intervals.icu — local or cloud.
- **Calendar & totals** — A visual training diary of the days you moved, plus lifetime totals with a few fun facts.
- **Training coach** ◆ — A readiness "traffic light" and a chat coach that reads your own data — offline for the basics.
- **HRV & wellness** ◆ — Heart-rate variability computed from the watch's raw beats; resting HR & weight from intervals.icu (in case you still use other device to track those metrics).
- **Gear tracker** ◆ — Track bikes, shoes and parts with maintenance reminders before something wears out on you.

### 03 — Keep it alive  _(sub-label: backup · repair)_
- **Backup & restore** — Safe backups of routes, POIs and firmware to a folder 
- **Firmware flasher** — Install firmware over USB
- **Heart-rate sensor** — Connect a Suunto HR chest strap and check its battery level and firmware version.
- **Interval builder** — A visual workout builder for structured intervals, turnable into an workout on the Ambit 3 or an app for Ambit 1/2.
- **Guided Interval Workouts** — a lost feature, now revived for the Ambit 3. With the addition of an Intervals.icu workout to ambit converter.
- **Kailash travel log** ◆ — Read travel history, countries & places visited, set a Home Location — far past its iPhone-only past.

## A FEW HONEST LIMITATIONS
- Eyebrow: No fine print
- Heading: **A few honest limitations.**
- Intro: Because you're trusting your watch to it — here's exactly where the edges are.
- **Bluetooth** — Works fully on Android. On the desktop app it's cable-only for now — reliable on the first pair, flaky after, so it ships as an opt-in experiment.
- **Firmware** — Sommet can flash firmware, and was tested on 5 different devices on different operative systems, but SuuntoLink is still the recommended tool. The flasher is just an emergency fallback/alternative.

- **Not yet back** — Training plans in the watch menu — aren't fully revived; a workaround is done, for Ambit 3, a guided workout with the name of the day.
- **Testing** — The app was tested and verified on real hardware: Suunto Ambit 1/2/3r/3s/3 Peak/Traver/Kailash. The rest of the range share its internals and are expected to work, but haven't all been confirmed.
- **As-is** — Provided with no warranty, for use on your own hardware at your own risk. If a watch ever misbehaves, SuuntoLink can always reset it.

## WHERE WE STAND
- Eyebrow: Where we stand
- Heading: **Grateful — not against Suunto.**
- Paragraph 1: Suunto has spent decades building outdoor instruments worth keeping, and their modern watches are genuinely excellent — SuuntoLink and the Suunto app serve them well. Sommet exists only for the older devices those tools have moved on from, so their owners have an easy, offline way to keep using them.
- Paragraph 2: Sommet is an independent, unofficial community project — not affiliated with, nor endorsed by, Suunto or Garmin. It builds on the openambit and opensportsync work, and hopes to give a little back to that community.
