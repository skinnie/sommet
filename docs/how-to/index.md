# How-to guides

Goal-oriented recipes for getting a specific thing done.

- [Build and run](build-and-run.md) — desktop and Android build/run commands.
- [Install Sommet](install.md) — the ready-made downloads for Linux, Android, Windows and macOS, the udev rule, and the macOS permission.
- [Bluetooth on Linux](bluetooth-on-linux.md) — what Bluetooth needs, pairing a watch, a Bluetooth mouse that stops working, and Bluetooth that stops answering.
- [Use the tools](using-the-tools.md) — everyday `tools/` commands and writing to a watch.
- [The library and your watches](routes-and-pois-library.md) — where routes and POIs are kept, and why the library and a watch's own list are independent.
- [Create a structured workout](create-a-structured-workout.md) — interval workouts in the watch's WORKOUT menu, and what to do when one won't install.
- [Keep a Bluetooth watch paired on Linux](bluetooth-pairing-on-linux.md) — how Sommet finishes a pairing on the watch, and the privacy switch for a watch it can't (the Kailash, for now).
