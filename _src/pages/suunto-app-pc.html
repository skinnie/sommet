On your computer

A Suunto app for your PC.

The Suunto app lives on the phone, and SuuntoLink only updates the watch. Sommet is a free desktop app for the older watches: plug in the cable and manage the watch from Windows, Linux or macOS, with no account and almost entirely offline.

What you can do from the computer

  • ActivitiesDownload them from the watch and see the map, charts and heart-rate zones. Export GPX or FIT, or send them to Strava, Runalyze, intervals.icu or Garmin Connect.
  • RoutesSend a GPX route with its map and climb, remove routes, and export the ones already on the watch.
  • POIsAdd them from a map search or coordinates, remove them, export them.
  • Sport modes and settingsEdit modes, data screens and the watch's settings, and copy them from one watch to another.
  • Keeping it workingGPS orbit data, the clock, backups and restore, the official PDF manual, and a firmware flasher for emergencies.

Install it

  • WindowsDownload the .zip, unzip it and double-click Sommet.exe. The first launch shows an “unverified developer” prompt: click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. Checked with a real Ambit2.
  • LinuxOne AppImage file: make it executable and run it. USB needs a udev rule, installed once; without it the app sees the watch but gets “Permission denied”. Built to run on older distros and older computers.
  • macOSDownload the .dmg and drag Sommet to Applications; on first launch right-click the app and choose Open. Tested with a watch over the cable. macOS asks for the Input Monitoring permission, because the watch is a USB HID device; allow it once.
  • AndroidInstall the APK and connect the watch with a USB-OTG cable, or over Bluetooth for the watches that have it.

Good to know

Which watches work?

Suunto Ambit, Ambit2, Ambit2 S and R, Ambit3 Peak, Sport, Run and Vertical, Traverse, Traverse Alpha and Kailash. The Spartan, Suunto 5, Suunto 9 and later watches are not supported: SuuntoLink and the Suunto app already serve them well.

Cable or Bluetooth?

On the computer, use the USB cable. Bluetooth on the desktop is experimental; on Android it works for the Ambit3, Traverse and Kailash.

Do I still need SuuntoLink?

Keep it for firmware updates and for resetting a watch that has frozen. Sommet does the day-to-day work.
