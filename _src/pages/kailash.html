The travel watch

An app for your Kailash.

The Kailash shipped iPhone-only, with very few options. Sommet gives it a home on Android or a computer, over USB and Bluetooth.

What Sommet does with a Kailash

  • Travel historyRead the watch's travel log and see the countries and places you have visited on a world map.
  • Home LocationSet the place the watch measures your distance and bearing from.
  • ActivitiesExport a recorded activity to GPX.
  • Settings and the clockRead and change the watch's settings, and set the time.
  • What it cannot doThe Kailash has no routes, so there is nothing to send there.

First aid

  • Dead batteryA replacement cell and a bit of patience does it. Plenty of teardown videos walk you through it.
  • Broken strapSuunto still sells replacement straps new, and other shops carry them too. A five-minute swap.
  • Frozen or stuck on “connect to Moveslink”Charge it overnight, connect and disconnect it once in SuuntoLink, then reset the firmware.
  • Lost the manualSommet downloads the official PDF manual for your watch from inside the app.
