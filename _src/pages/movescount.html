After Movescount

Looking for the Movescount login?

Movescount.com is closed, so there is no login to find, and Moveslink has nothing left to connect to. The watches themselves are fine. Here is what does each job today.

What replaced what

  • Firmware updates and resetsSuuntoLink, Suunto's own free tool for Windows and Mac. Keep it installed; it is also how you revive a watch stuck on the “connect to Moveslink” icon.
  • Routes and POIsSommet sends GPX routes and points of interest to the watch, and exports the ones already on it.
  • Sport modes and data screensSommet has a full editor: rename modes, set auto-lap and heart-rate limits, choose sensors, and pick the fields on each screen.
  • Your activitiesSommet downloads them to your own computer or phone, shows the map and charts, exports GPX or FIT, and can send them on to Strava, Runalyze, intervals.icu or Garmin Connect.
  • Planned workoutsOn the Ambit3 the watch's own WORKOUT menu is back, fed by a Training Program from intervals.icu or made in the app. The Ambit and Ambit2 use App Zone apps.
  • GPS orbit data and the clockSommet updates the satellite data so the watch finds a fix faster, and sets the time, including for another time zone.

Questions

Is there an account?

No. Sommet has no account, no login and no subscription. It runs on your own machine and works almost entirely offline.

Are my old Movescount activities in Sommet?

No. Sommet reads what is on the watch and what you import; it has no access to the old Movescount site.

Is this against Suunto?

No. Sommet exists because these watches are worth keeping in use. For firmware we recommend Suunto's own SuuntoLink.
