Routes in, activities out

Put a GPX route on your Ambit.

Plan the route wherever you like, save it as a GPX file, and Sommet sends it to the watch so you can follow it on your wrist. The routes already on the watch are kept.

Sending a route

  • 1 · Open the GPX fileSommet shows a preview map of exactly what the route will look like, with its climb, before anything is written.
  • 2 · Send itOver the USB cable from a computer, or from an Android phone by cable or Bluetooth. It works on the Ambit, Ambit2, Ambit3 and Traverse. The Kailash has no routes.
  • 3 · Follow itWhere the route crosses or doubles back on itself, Sommet adds direction pins such as “Left 1.2”, and the watch announces them in order as you approach.
  • Reading routes backRoutes stored on the watch can be read off and saved as GPX files, and removed when you no longer need them.

Getting activities out

  • GPX or FITDownload an activity from the watch and export it in either format.
  • Strava and othersSend activities straight to Strava, Runalyze, intervals.icu or Garmin Connect.
  • Points of interestAdd POIs from a map search or coordinates, and export the ones on the watch. A Traverse gets its own 48 POI types.
