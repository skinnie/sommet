An app for your Ambit3.

The Ambit3 is the most thoroughly tested watch in Sommet. It connects over the USB cable on any computer, and over Bluetooth from an Android phone.

What Sommet does with an Ambit3

  • ActivitiesDownload them and see the map, charts and heart-rate zones. Export GPX or FIT, or send them to Strava, Runalyze, intervals.icu or Garmin Connect.
  • Routes and POIsSend a GPX route with its map and climb, add POIs from a map search or coordinates, and export what is on the watch.
  • Sport modes and settingsEdit modes, data screens, apps and the watch's settings, and copy them from one watch to another.
  • Planned workoutsThe watch's own WORKOUT menu is back, fed by a Training Program from intervals.icu or made in the app.
  • Keeping it workingGPS and GLONASS orbit data, the clock, backups and restore, and a firmware flasher for emergencies.

Connecting

  • USB cableWorks on Windows, Linux, macOS and Android (with a USB-OTG adapter).
  • BluetoothWorks on Android. On the desktop it is experimental.

First aid

  • Dead batteryA replacement cell and a bit of patience does it. Plenty of teardown videos walk you through it.
  • Broken strapSuunto still sells replacement straps new, and other shops carry them too. A five-minute swap.
  • Frozen or stuck on “connect to Moveslink”Charge it overnight, connect and disconnect it once in SuuntoLink, then reset the firmware.
  • Lost the manualSommet downloads the official PDF manual for your watch from inside the app.
