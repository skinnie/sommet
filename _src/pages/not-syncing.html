When it won't sync

Your watch won't sync. The cable still works.

A watch that refuses to pair over Bluetooth has not lost anything: the activities are still in its memory, and the USB cable does not depend on pairing. Sommet reads and writes the watch over that cable, from a computer or from an Android phone with a USB-OTG adapter.

What to try

  • Use the cablePlug the watch into a computer, open Sommet, and it recognises the watch by itself: model, serial number, firmware version and battery level. From there you can download every activity.
  • On AndroidConnect the watch to the phone with a USB-OTG adapter, or pair it over Bluetooth from Sommet's own settings. Bluetooth works on Android for the Ambit3, Traverse and Kailash.
  • On Linux, “Permission denied”The app sees the watch but cannot open it until a udev rule is installed, once. The install page has the two commands; unplug and replug the watch afterwards.
  • On macOS, slow or missing dataAllow Sommet under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Input Monitoring, then relaunch it. macOS asks for this for every USB HID device.
  • Stuck on the “connect to Moveslink” iconCharge the watch overnight, connect and disconnect it once in SuuntoLink, then reset the firmware. That clears it in almost every case.
  • Frozen or behaving strangelyConnect it to SuuntoLink on a Mac or Windows computer and reset it. Your settings are lost but the watch comes back.

Once it is connected

Where do my activities go?

Onto your own computer or phone. From there you can export GPX or FIT files, or send activities to Strava, Runalyze, intervals.icu or Garmin Connect.

Can I still send routes?

Yes: GPX routes go to the Ambit, Ambit2, Ambit3 and Traverse over the cable. The Kailash has no routes.
